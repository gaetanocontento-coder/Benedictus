Build a full-stack web application called "Benedictus — Regula Humanitatis" for an Italian platform that translates 1,500 years of Benedictine wisdom into a modern leadership and community-building methodology. The platform serves both B2C (individuals) and B2B (companies/institutions).

---

## TECH STACK
- Frontend: React + Tailwind CSS
- Backend: Node.js + Express
- Database: PostgreSQL (with Prisma ORM)
- Auth: JWT-based authentication with membership tiers
- Payments: Stripe integration (subscription + one-time)
- Email: Nodemailer or Resend for newsletter
- Storage: Cloudinary or S3 for media assets

---

## VISUAL IDENTITY & TONE
- Aesthetic: monastic, minimal, evocative — dark parchment tones (#1a1208, #f5e6c8), serif typography (e.g. Playfair Display for headings, Inter for body)
- Latin year header "Anno Domini MCCCXXVII" as a recurring visual motif
- Language: Italian (primary), English (secondary)
- Tone: not instructional from above, but invitational — "cammino" not "corso", "custode" not "leader"

## PAGES & SECTIONS (from the document)

### 1. Homepage
- Hero with manifesto text: "Per 1500 anni, i monasteri hanno custodito la civiltà quando il mondo crollava. Oggi il mondo non crolla, ma l'umano sì."
- CTA: "Inizia il Cammino" → leads to newsletter signup or registration
- Three rhetorical questions (loneliness at work, emptiness despite success, fragmented community sense)
- Section: "La Crisi Silenziosa della Leadership" with key stats (OMS burnout data, Great Resignation)

### 2. Chi Siamo
- Not an organization, but a community
- The 4 transformative steps: DISARMARE → ASCOLTARE → CUSTODIRE → RIGENERARE

### 3. La Chiamata
- The "Perché Ora?" manifesto section
- Market positioning: not competing in Leadership Development (€366B market), but creating a new category — "rigenerazione comunitaria"
- Blue Ocean framing

### 4. Il Manifesto
- Long-form editorial page: rebuild communities, orient toward the common good

### 5. La Regula
- The Benedictine principles translated into modern equivalents:
  * Ora et Labora → Anti-burnout design
  * Capitolo → Participatory governance
  * Stabilitas → Mutual commitment
  * Abbas → Servant leadership
  * Hospitalitas → Inclusion practices

### 6. Lectio Humanitatis
- Content archive: articles/reflections, 3 free/month for Pellegrino tier
- Full access for paid tiers
- Category tags: Ascolto, Comunità, Ritmo, Umiltà, Custodia

### 7. Abbas — Podcast/Video
- Video podcast page: embed player, episode list, episode descriptions
- "La Voce dei Maestri" — wise voices from tradition speaking to the present
- Episode format: Lectio + conversation + community reflections

### 8. Percorso Formativo
- The learning journey: 6 online modules (2 months each) + 3 residential weekends + final project + certification
- Visual progress tracker (pilgrimage metaphor — stations/waypoints)
- Cohort system (max 30 people)

### 9. Cura dell'Anima
- Digital library / resource shelf
- Books, readings, curated resources

### 10. Officina della Sapienza
- Workshop/lab registration page
- Collective work sessions — theory into practice
- Calendar view of upcoming workshops

### 11. Celluzza
- Guided meditation / interiority section (audio/video)
- Private, intimate UI (dim background, centered content, minimal chrome)

### 12. Testimonianze
- Stories of transformation
- Quote cards + photo + short bio
- "Il Voto del Custode" ritual section: display the full vow text, graduates who have taken it

### 13. Restiamo in Contatto
- Newsletter signup: name + email + optional "perché sei qui?" text field
- Confirmation with welcoming message

## MEMBERSHIP SYSTEM (3 Tiers)

### Tier 1 — Pellegrino (Free)
- Weekly newsletter
- Abbas podcast access
- 3 Lectio Humanitatis/month
- Read-only community access
- Goal: 5,000+ subscribers in Year 1

### Tier 2 — Monaco (Paid, monthly or annual)
- Everything in Tier 1 +
- Full Lectio archive
- 2 live masterclasses/month
- Active community (write + groups)
- Celluzza guided meditation sessions
- Digital resource library
- Target: 200 members Year 1

### Tier 3 — Abbas (Premium, monthly or annual)
- Everything in Tier 2 +
- 1 individual coaching session/quarter
- Priority access to physical events
- Alumni mentorship
- Annual certification
- Annual residential retreat included
- Target: 50 members Year 1

Implement Stripe subscriptions for Monaco and Abbas tiers. Free signup for Pellegrino. Gated content based on tier level.

## DATABASE SCHEMA (key models)

- Episode (Abbas Podcast): id, title, description, embedUrl, publishedAt
- Workshop (Officina): id, title, date, location, maxParticipants, currentParticipants
- Testimonial: id, quote, authorName, authorRole, photo
- NewsletterSubscriber: id, email, name, whyHere, createdAt
- CohortEnrollment: id, userId, cohortId, status, startDate
